### Significance and Novelty of the Study
How high-stakes national security scandals erupt and resolve in the digital era is a defining question of contemporary political communication. In early November 2026, an alleged illegal electronic wiretapping scandal targeting Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto triggered an acute institutional crisis involving the executive cabinet, senior police generals, parliamentary oversight committees, and digital public spheres.

Our study makes three substantial theoretical and methodological contributions:
1. **Advancing the Third-Level Network Agenda-Setting (NAS) Model:** While extant agenda-setting literature predominantly explores electoral campaigns in Western contexts, our paper provides empirical proof of an **"Oscillating Reciprocity / Two-Phase Reversal Flow"** in crisis communication: a bottom-up phase where decentralized digital discourse (*dark social* and social media platform X) bypasses professional journalistic gatekeeping to force issue salience, followed by a top-down institutional domestication phase wherein mainstream press and elite actors reclaim attribute hegemony post-official press conference.
2. **Computational Social Network Analysis & Narrative Brokerage:** Leveraging bipartite graph modeling and NodeXL Pro, we reveal a *bipolar core-periphery network structure* ($Q = 0.3335$) and quantify how constitutional-legal narrative brokers (such as Coordinating Minister Yusril Ihza Mahendra, $C_B = 0.0741$) bridge structural holes between conflicting security institutions, channeling acute political volatility into institutionalized judicial due process.
3. **Open Science & Methodological Rigor:** The study strictly adheres to the **PRISMA 2020** corpus curation protocol across a verified multi-tiered corpus ($N = 200$ primary articles, $N = 114$ independent replication stream from World A.LIVE, and $N = 15,420$ digital public interactions). Cross-correlation function (CCF) analysis demonstrates a statistically significant contemporaneous peak correlation at Lag 0 ($r = 0.896, p = 0.0005$).
